What we'll ask

Five questions. That's where we begin.

  1. What does your business do?
    What you provide, who your customers are and where you operate.
  2. What takes too much time or money?
    Repetitive tasks, administration, enquiries or other bottlenecks.
  3. What frustrates your customers?
    Delays, confusion, common questions or avoidable problems.
  4. What would you most like to improve?
    Think about the next twelve months.
  5. What must not be compromised?
    Privacy, personal service, accuracy, control, affordability or something else.

Please don't share passwords, confidential customer records or sensitive personal information.
