Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

AI Business Ready respects your privacy. We collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to enquiries and provide our services.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, business name, website and information about your business and its needs.

If you purchase an assessment, we may also receive payment confirmation and transaction details from our payment provider.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, assess whether our service is suitable, communicate with you, prepare your report and maintain necessary business records.

We do not sell customer information or automatically add customers to marketing lists.

3. Use of artificial intelligence

We may use AI-assisted tools to research, analyse information and help prepare reports. Recommendations are reviewed before delivery.

Information submitted to external AI services may be processed or stored by those providers, including outside Australia.

We aim to minimise the information shared with AI services and avoid supplying unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Please do not submit passwords, confidential customer records, financial account details or sensitive personal information through our enquiry form.

4. Service providers

We use third-party services to operate our website, receive enquiries, communicate by email, process payments and prepare assessments.

These providers may process information in different countries under their own applicable privacy and security arrangements.

5. Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not collect or store your full payment card number through our website.

6. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse and loss.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver our service, manage enquiries, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You may contact us to ask about personal information we hold, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Contact us

For questions, privacy concerns or requests concerning your information, please use our enquiry page and identify your message as a privacy enquiry.

We will review your request and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services and systems evolve. The latest version will be published on this website.
